About Diverse RecruitingPeople first. Progress always.

Creating space
for possibility.

Opportunity opens
through conversation.

Talent has never belonged to one background, one network, or one path.

Diverse Recruiting helps organizations find people whose experience and potential deserve a closer look. We combine recruitment, career support, and workplace consultancy to connect opportunity with a broader range of talent.

Our work starts by listening. We take time to understand the needs of a team, the ambitions of a candidate, and the conditions that help both succeed.

Founder & Principal Consultant · United States

Mohammed
Xavier Brown.

Mohammed Xavier Brown founded Diverse Recruiting to make hiring more thoughtful and opportunity more accessible.

His approach starts with the people on both sides of a search: what organizations need to build, and what candidates need to do their best work. That means clear conversations, a wider view of talent, and support that keeps people at the center.

Through recruitment, career support, and workplace consultancy, Mohammed brings that approach to organizations across the Northeast and beyond.

About the organization

Headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, Northwind Solar is a renewable energy company specializing in solar power solutions and sustainable energy infrastructure. The company focuses on advancing the adoption of clean energy through innovative solar technologies and projects.

The opportunity

Northwind Solar is seeking a Human Resources Specialist to support its growing workforce and strengthen the organization's people operations. The successful candidate will contribute to talent acquisition, employee onboarding, HR administration, employee relations, and organizational development while supporting a collaborative and inclusive workplace culture.

Key responsibilities

  • Coordinate recruitment, hiring, and employee onboarding.
  • Support employee relations and HR policy implementation.
  • Maintain accurate personnel records and HR information systems.
  • Assist with benefits administration and employee development initiatives.
  • Support compliance with applicable employment laws and regulations.
  • Collaborate with department leaders on workforce planning and employee engagement.

What you bring

  • Relevant experience in human resources or people operations.
  • Knowledge of HR policies, recruitment processes, and employment practices.
  • Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience with HR information systems preferred.

Jobs/Obsidian Energy

Executive Leadership · Obsidian Energy

Chief Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Officer

Clean Energy & Data Center Infrastructure

About the organization

Obsidian Energy is a clean-energy startup specializing in hydrothermal energy solutions for data centers. Operating across New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Massachusetts, the company focuses on supporting energy-intensive digital infrastructure through sustainable energy technologies and resource management.

The opportunity

Obsidian Energy is seeking a Chief Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Officer to lead its enterprise-wide DEI strategy and organizational inclusion initiatives. Working closely with executive leadership, this senior-level professional will help shape the company's organizational culture, strengthen equitable talent management practices, and integrate inclusive leadership principles across the business.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and oversee the company's enterprise-wide diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy.
  • Partner with executive leadership on organizational culture and workforce initiatives.
  • Establish measurable inclusion and employee engagement objectives.
  • Advise on equitable recruitment, advancement, and talent development practices.
  • Lead internal education, leadership development, and organizational inclusion programs.
  • Monitor workforce trends and communicate DEI-related performance indicators to senior leadership.
  • Coordinate with legal and human resources teams to ensure initiatives comply with applicable laws.

What you bring

  • Significant senior leadership experience in DEI, human resources, organizational development, or related functions.
  • Demonstrated ability to develop and implement enterprise-wide organizational strategies.
  • Experience advising executive teams and leading cross-functional initiatives.
  • Strong knowledge of organizational development, talent management, and employment compliance.
  • Excellent executive communication, strategic planning, and stakeholder engagement skills.

Obsidian Energy

Clean Energy & Data Center Infrastructure
Company locations
New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Massachusetts
